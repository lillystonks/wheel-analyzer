Wheel Analyzer
Cash-secured puts · covered calls · live chains

Is this a good wheel trade?

Pick a stock and an expiry. The app pulls the live option chain, works the numbers the way a wheel trader would, scores the trade, and shows you the strikes and expiries that look better.

Assumptions

Drift is the yearly move you assume for the share price when estimating probabilities — 0% is the neutral default and keeps the numbers close to what the option market itself implies. Leave the rate blank to use the live 13-week T-bill yield.

Data comes from Yahoo Finance through your own Cloudflare Worker and is typically delayed about 15 minutes. US equities and ETFs with listed options work best.

One-time setup: connect a data proxy

GitHub Pages can't call Yahoo Finance directly (no CORS, and Yahoo rate-limits by IP). A tiny free Cloudflare Worker sits in between. You deploy it once, then paste its URL here.

  1. Install the CLI: npm i -g wrangler (needs Node).
  2. From the repo: cd worker && wrangler deploy — it opens a browser to sign in, then prints a URL ending in .workers.dev.
  3. Click Settings (top right) and paste that URL.

Full walkthrough, including the no-terminal route, is in SETUP.md.
